A `VirtualList` or `VirtualListView` whose scroll bar is dragged or jumped far into rows it hasn't loaded now loads the rows at the viewport in one query of up to ten pages, rather than a page at a time from the loaded rows, which showed blank space for a frame or a round trip a page. A layout pass that ran out with a snapshot not laid out yet now gets another one, where before the list could stand blank until the next scroll. A list that sticks to its bottom no longer follows it while a jump looks up its row, as a chat opening on its latest message does: it paged through every row the client had, a new query each pass, and the jump waited behind them. A scroll once a jump's row is on screen is no longer put back on the row until the server confirms the rows around it.

A list restored from `scrollState` now opens where it was, with the row that was at the top at the same height, in its first frame, from the rows the client already has, and stays there as the server confirms them. It used to open off by the rows above it measured since, or, for a list opened away from its start, at its bottom. A list that sticks to its bottom and was there reopens at its bottom, following the rows that came while it was closed. A saved state's `anchor` is now the page from the row at the top, and its `scrollOffset` is counted from that row. `LoopbackServer` now serves a client that kept its rows across launches, as with SQLite storage, from the cookie it has.
